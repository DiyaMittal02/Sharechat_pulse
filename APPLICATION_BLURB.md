### Longer (for a cover-letter paragraph)

To show how I'd approach the Product Analyst role, I built **Pulse** — a content & creator intelligence platform for a ShareChat-style multi-language social feed, deployed and running on a reproducible synthetic dataset I generated. The analytics side demonstrates the fundamentals: one source-of-truth metric layer so every team reads a number the same way, benchmarks computed per language × genre (so a Tamil romance short is judged against its real peers, not a global mean), DAU-weighted cohort aggregation, and a moderation funnel analyzed for stage drop-off and per-language fairness. I also validated a breakout-detection score with precision/recall against ground truth, and documented findings in SQL and a written insights report. On top of that I wrote a full **product teardown** of ShareChat/Moj — personas, growth loop, a North-Star metric tree, competitive positioning, monetization trade-offs, and a RICE-prioritized set of recommendations, each with a success metric and a guardrail. The goal was to connect data to decisions, which is the core of the role. (All figures are clearly labeled synthetic/illustrative — no real ShareChat data is used or implied.)
